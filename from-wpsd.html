The dashboard

Change your talkgroups directly on the hotspot.

Presets

Save setups like “Everyday” and “Net night”, and switch with one tap. Only the difference is changed.

Timed statics

Enter your Thursday net talkgroup in local time, and it only turns on during the net.

RX offset tuning

Key up a few times while it steps the offset. It keeps the offset with the lowest BER after asking you.

Stop call, drop dynamic

Clear a stuck route straight from your phone, after a quick confirmation prompt.

Moving over

Copy five numbers, then swap your cards.

Start with a new SD card and keep your current one safe. Copy these details from your current hotspot into the wizard. Your radio’s codeplug stays exactly the same.

  1. Write Raspberry Pi OS Lite (64-bit) to the new card
  2. Run the wizard and type in your numbers
  3. Swap the cards and power up. Give it 5 to 15 minutes.
This card suits you if
  • You only use DMR, and strictly on BrandMeister
  • You change talkgroups often, especially before nets
  • You want a dashboard that reads easily on a phone
  • You prefer setting things up once on your computer
Stay on WPSD if
  • You use D-Star, YSF, P25, NXDN or M17
  • You connect to TGIF, FreeDMR or other DMR networks
  • Your hotspot is a Pi 5, which we do not support yet
  • You need proven software, as this project is still early

Try it out on a spare card first.

Your current card stays exactly as it is, so you can swap back anytime.
