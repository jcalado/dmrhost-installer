The dashboard

The talkgroup page you keep opening SelfCare for, on the hotspot.

Presets

Save “Everyday” and “Net night”, switch in one tap. Only the difference is changed.

Timed statics

Your Thursday net TG, on only on Thursdays, entered in local time.

RX offset tuning

Key up a few times; it steps the offset and keeps the one with the lowest BER, after asking.

Stop call, drop dynamic

Clear a stuck route from your phone, with a confirm first.

Moving over

Write down five numbers, then swap cards.

Use a new SD card and keep your current one as a fallback. Copy these from your current hotspot’s configuration page into the wizard. Your radio’s codeplug doesn’t change.

  1. Write Raspberry Pi OS Lite (64-bit) to the new card
  2. Run the wizard and enter the numbers
  3. Swap cards and power up. 5 to 15 minutes.
This card suits you if
  • You only use DMR, and only BrandMeister
  • You change talkgroups often, or before nets
  • You want a dashboard that’s easy to read on your phone
  • You’d rather set it up once, on your computer
Stay on WPSD if
  • You use D-Star, YSF, P25, NXDN or M17
  • You connect to TGIF, FreeDMR or other DMR networks
  • Your hotspot is a Pi 5, which isn’t supported here yet
  • You need something proven: this project is early

Try it on a spare card.

Your current card stays as it is. Swap back any time.
